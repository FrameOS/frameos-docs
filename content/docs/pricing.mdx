**FrameOS is free and open source.** All of it: the software that runs on your frame, the backend
you can host at home, the scene editor, the apps, and the code behind FrameOS Cloud itself. It is
licensed under the AGPL-3.0 and lives at [github.com/FrameOS/frameos](https://github.com/FrameOS/frameos).
You can read it, change it and run it without asking anyone or paying anything.

So the short version of this page is: **you never have to pay to use FrameOS.**

## Three ways to run it, all free

| | What it is | Cost |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Standalone](/guide/standalone) | A frame on its own. Flash an SD card or an ESP32 and it runs, with no account and no server. | Free |
| [Self-hosted backend](/guide/backend) | A web app you run at home that manages all your frames. No limits but your own hardware. | Free |
| [FrameOS Cloud](/guide/cloud) | We host the backend for you at [cloud.frameos.net](https://cloud.frameos.net). | Free for a normal home, paid above that |

Nothing is held back from the free paths. A standalone or self-hosted frame gets every app, every
scene, the editor and every update. If the cloud disappeared tomorrow, your frames would keep
showing what they show and the software to run them yourself would still be public.

## FrameOS Cloud is mostly free

The cloud exists for people who would rather not host anything. You make an account, link a frame
and manage it from the browser.

The **Free plan** is meant to be all a household needs, for as long as you like:

- 20 frames, with 20 scenes on each
- 250 MB of [photos](/guide/photos)
- 100 MB each of private scenes, backups and frame logs
- The scene store, the scene editor, photo albums and backups
- Updates, two-factor sign-in, and everything else that keeps your frames safe

No card is asked for, and nothing on that list has a time limit.

## We charge when usage gets big

Hosting is the one part of FrameOS that costs money every month: servers, storage, bandwidth and
backups. One home's worth of frames costs very little. A wall of 150 screens, or tens of gigabytes
of photos, does not.

So the paid plans are about **scale, not features**. A higher plan buys room for more frames and
more files. It does not unlock a better editor or hide anything from free accounts.

The rule behind the table is simple: a plan at $N a month holds N times the Free plan's storage.
Business also comes with priority support, and dedicated support at $499.

Planned for the paid plans, and **not part of what you pay for today**: sharing frames with other
people on your plan, virtual frames and thin clients such as the Inky Frame, and single sign-on
with SAML for Business. They will be listed here as included once they exist.

## Paying is how you support FrameOS

FrameOS is a bootstrapped project. There are no investors behind it, no ads in it, and nothing
about you or your frames is sold to anyone. The servers and the time that goes into the software
are paid for by the people who choose a paid plan.

That makes a paid plan two things at once. If you run a lot of frames, it is a fair price for what
you use. And if you simply like FrameOS and want it to keep getting better, it is the most direct
way to say so, even at $2 a month and even if the Free plan would have been enough for you.

There are other ways to help that cost nothing, and they matter just as much:

- Star the project on [GitHub](https://github.com/FrameOS/frameos), report bugs and send fixes.
- Publish the scenes you make to the [scene store](https://scenes.frameos.net).
- Show your frame and help others on [Discord](https://discord.gg/9dT9y7EzUw).

## How paying works

- **Where:** paid plans are being opened up now. You choose one under **Settings → Usage** in your
  [cloud account](https://cloud.frameos.net/account/usage). If checkout is not switched on for your
  account yet, the button there writes to us and we set the plan up with you.
- **Billing:** monthly, in advance, in US dollars. VAT or sales tax is added at checkout where it
  applies, and you see the total before you pay.
- **Who charges you:** card payments go through [Creem](https://creem.io), a merchant of record.
  Creem is the seller for the payment, sends your receipt and invoice, and is the name on your card
  statement. We never see your card.
- **Changing plan:** a bigger plan starts at once and you pay the difference for the rest of the
  month. A smaller one starts at your next renewal.
- **Cancelling:** any time, from the same Usage page. The plan runs to the end of the month you
  paid for and is not charged again.

## What about AI?

The scene editor has an AI assistant that writes and edits scenes for you. It works on every plan
and costs the same on each. While FrameOS Cloud is in beta it is free, within a daily allowance.
You can also plug in your own OpenAI key, or switch AI off for your account.

On a self-hosted backend the AI runs on your own key, so there is nothing to pay us at all.

## What stays free

Some things will not move behind a paid plan: making an account, enrolling and managing the frames
you own, installing public scenes from the store, and anything that is about security (two-factor
sign-in, revoking a frame, signed updates). And FrameOS itself stays open source.
