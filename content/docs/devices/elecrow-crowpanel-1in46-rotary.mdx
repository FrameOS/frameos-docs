The **Elecrow CrowPanel 1.46" rotary** is a round 360×360 IPS **touch LCD** set in a knob, with an
ESP32-S3 (16&nbsp;MB flash, 8&nbsp;MB PSRAM). Turn the knob, push it, or tap the glass.

Only the circle inside the square is glass, so FrameOS treats it as a
[round screen](/guide/scenes#round-screens): the scene switcher, the scene settings, the frame
page, the keyboard on the screen and the edge swipes all stay inside the circle, and so do most
sample scenes. The [scene store](/guide/scene-store) marks the scenes that suit a round screen and
offers them first.

## What it does

Pick the **Elecrow CrowPanel 1.46" rotary** hardware preset (`elecrow_crowpanel_rotary_1in46`):

- **Scenes animate and answer a touch.** It's an [LCD-class screen](/guide/esp32#lcd): the panel
  keeps its own picture and is sent only the part that changed.
- **Touch** reaches scenes as taps, drags, swipes and pointer events
  ([input events](/guide/scenes#input)), and a swipe in from the rim is the frame's: up for the
  scene switcher, down for the scene's settings, left and right for back and forward.
- **The knob** ([button standard](/guide/buttons)): each click of a turn is Next or
  Prev, walking what's on the screen - or, on a scene with nothing to press, its own forward and
  back. Pushed, it's the Action key, and held it opens the scene switcher. The push is the whole
  glass pressed down, so it's also a tap wherever your finger lands: to push without tapping
  anything, press on a spot with nothing to tap.
- **BOOT** turns the screen off or on, and held it opens the scene switcher.
- **Brightness and dimming.** The backlight dims: set **Brightness**, **Dim when idle** and
  **Dimmed brightness** under [Power](/guide/settings#power-and-battery), or drag the Brightness
  bar on the frame's own page.

The board has no microphone, so no voice chat. The ring of eight LEDs isn't driven yet, and the
battery isn't read: its divider sits on a pin the ESP32 can't read while WiFi is on.

## Setup

1. In the FrameOS backend or [FrameOS Cloud](/guide/cloud), create a frame with **Flash embedded
   device** - or flash it standalone from [flash.frameos.net](https://flash.frameos.net/).
2. Choose **ESP32-S3**, select the **Elecrow CrowPanel 1.46" rotary** preset, and enter your WiFi
   details.
3. Flash it over USB from the browser.
4. Install scenes as usual - they sync over WiFi, and the device updates itself over the air.
