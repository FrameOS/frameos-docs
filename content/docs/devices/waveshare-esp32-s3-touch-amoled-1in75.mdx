The **Waveshare ESP32-S3-Touch-AMOLED-1.75** is a round watch-sized display: a 1.75" 466×466
**AMOLED** with capacitive touch, driven by an ESP32-S3 with 16&nbsp;MB of flash and 8&nbsp;MB of
PSRAM. Beside the screen it has a speaker and two microphones, a battery gauge, a clock chip, an
accelerometer and a TF slot. The **-B** is the same board in a case, and takes the same preset.

Only the circle inside the square is glass, so FrameOS treats it as a
[round screen](/guide/scenes#round-screens): the scene switcher, the scene settings, the frame
page, the keyboard on the screen, the edge swipes and the loading mark all stay inside the circle,
and so do the frame's own apps - the Scene store, Files, Terminal and Code editor. Most sample
scenes - the clocks, the games, the tools and the remotes - lay out for the circle, and the
[scene store](/guide/scene-store) marks the scenes that suit a round screen and offers them first.

## What it does

Pick the **Waveshare ESP32-S3-Touch-AMOLED-1.75** hardware preset
(`waveshare_esp32_s3_touch_amoled_1_75`) and:

- **Scenes animate and answer a touch.** It's an [LCD-class screen](/guide/esp32#lcd): the scene
  draws into a frame buffer, only the rectangle that changed goes to the panel, and the panel
  keeps its own picture, so a flash write never tears it and there's no *Updating* screen while a
  push is stored.
- **Touch** reaches scenes as taps, drags, swipes and pointer events
  ([input events](/guide/scenes#input)), and a swipe in from the rim is the frame's: up for the
  scene switcher, down for the scene's settings, left and right for back and forward.
- **Two keys**: PWR pressed turns the screen off or on, pressed twice opens the scene switcher;
  BOOT held is the [voice chat](/guide/esp32#voice-chat) key.
- **A touch wakes it** from deep sleep, like a key.
- **The accelerometer** answers on the console (`imu`) and in the frame's hardware reports.

## Not tried yet

The TF slot, the speaker and the microphones are wired up in the preset but haven't been tried
on the board yet, so the voice chat on BOOT is untested.

## Setup

1. In the FrameOS backend or [FrameOS Cloud](/guide/cloud), create a frame with **Flash embedded
   device** - or flash it standalone from [flash.frameos.net](https://flash.frameos.net/).
2. Choose **ESP32-S3**, select the **Waveshare ESP32-S3-Touch-AMOLED-1.75** preset, and enter your
   WiFi details.
3. Flash it over USB from the browser. A touch board sets up on its own screen: swipe down from
   the top, tap the gear, and join a WiFi network there.
4. Install scenes as usual - they sync over WiFi, and the device updates itself over the air.

## Status

🟢 **Confirmed working** - tested on real hardware: the picture, taps, drags and edge swipes,
both keys, the clock, the battery gauge, the audio codec and the accelerometer, and the frame page
used to join WiFi from the screen.
