The **Seeed reTerminal D1001** is the first of Seeed's LCD reTerminals: an 8" 800×1280 capacitive
touch screen on MIPI-DSI, driven by an **ESP32-P4** (dual-core RISC-V at 400&nbsp;MHz) with
32&nbsp;MB of PSRAM and an ESP32-C6 for Wi-Fi 6 and Bluetooth, plus a 2&nbsp;MP camera, two
microphones, a speaker, a microSD slot and a 2500&nbsp;mAh battery.

## Status

⚪ **Not supported yet.** The FrameOS firmware is built for the ESP32-S3 and ESP32-C3 and drives
e-paper panels. An ESP32-P4 build with a MIPI-DSI LCD driver is a port we haven't done yet. With
32&nbsp;MB of PSRAM the board has the memory for it, and full-color LCD scenes are something FrameOS
already renders on a [Raspberry Pi over HDMI](/devices/framebuffer-hdmi).

Want it? Say so on [GitHub](https://github.com/FrameOS/frameos/issues) or
[Discord](https://discord.gg/9dT9y7EzUw) - it helps us decide what to port next.
