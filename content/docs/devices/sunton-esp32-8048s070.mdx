The **Sunton ESP32-8048S070** (also sold under the Guition name) is a cheap 7" 800×480 IPS
**touch LCD** with an ESP32-S3-WROOM-1 on the back: 16&nbsp;MB of flash, 8&nbsp;MB of PSRAM, a
GT911 touch panel and a microSD slot.

## What it does

Pick the **Sunton ESP32-8048S070** hardware preset (`sunton_esp32_8048s070`) and it behaves like
the other [LCD boards](/guide/esp32#lcd):

- **Scenes animate**: a scene whose render interval is under a second renders back to back, drawn
  straight into the LCD's frame buffer with only the changed part redrawn.
- **Touch** reaches scenes as taps, drags, swipes and pointer events
  ([input events](/guide/scenes#input)), so a [JSX](/guide/apps#jsx-uis) dashboard or remote
  answers a finger.
- **The BOOT key and the edges** come bound to the frame
  ([button standard](/guide/buttons)): BOOT pressed turns the screen off or on, held
  it opens the scene switcher; swipe up from the bottom for the switcher, down from the top for
  the scene's settings, and in from the left or right for back and forward.
- **The microSD slot** holds the frame's [assets](/guide/esp32#assets-and-the-sd-card) and fonts.
- **It boots straight into its scenes** and joins WiFi in the background; set the network on the
  screen (swipe down, tap the gear).

Its USB port is a CH340 serial bridge, which the browser flasher reads like any other.

## Setup

1. In the FrameOS backend or [FrameOS Cloud](/guide/cloud), create a frame with **Flash embedded
   device** - or flash it standalone from [flash.frameos.net](https://flash.frameos.net/).
2. Choose **ESP32-S3**, select the **Sunton ESP32-8048S070** preset, and enter your WiFi details.
3. Flash it over USB from the browser.
4. Install scenes as usual - they sync over WiFi, and the device updates itself over the air.
