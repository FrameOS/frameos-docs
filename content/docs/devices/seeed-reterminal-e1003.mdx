The **Seeed reTerminal E1003** is the 10.3" model in the reTerminal E-series: a 1404×1872
**16-level grayscale** panel with touch, an ESP32-S3 with 8&nbsp;MB of PSRAM and 32&nbsp;MB of
flash, a microSD slot, a 3000&nbsp;mAh battery, the E-series keys (a green refresh button and page
up/down), a buzzer, a temperature and humidity sensor and a microphone. Sixteen grays at that
density make it the reading and dashboard frame of the family.

From firmware 2026.10.21 FrameOS runs on it as a full [ESP32-S3 frame](/guide/esp32), rendering
scenes on the device. Pick the **Seeed reTerminal E1003** hardware preset
(`seeed_reterminal_e1003`) when you add the frame and it sets up:

- **The panel**: the 10.3" glass sits behind an IT8951 controller - the same chip and glass as the
  [Waveshare 10.3" HAT](/devices/waveshare-epd-10in3) - tuned for this board. Between refreshes
  the controller keeps its picture, so only the rows that changed are sent: a whole frame is about
  half a second on the wire, a clock's digits a few milliseconds. The board's temperature sensor
  feeds the refresh, as the panel needs.
- **Memory**: a grayscale panel needs no color, so the scene canvas takes one byte per pixel -
  2.5&nbsp;MB for 1872×1404 - and leaves the 8&nbsp;MB of PSRAM room for scenes and apps.
- **Touch**: taps, long presses and swipes reach scenes like a Pi touchscreen's
  ([input events](/guide/scenes#input)), and [JSX interfaces](/guide/apps#jsx-uis) can be tapped.
- **Keys and voice**: REFRESH, LEFT and RIGHT on GPIO 3, 4 and 5. Out of the box LEFT held is the
  previous scene, RIGHT held the next, and REFRESH held is the [voice chat](/guide/esp32#voice-chat):
  hold it, say what you want, let go. Change them under
  [button actions](/guide/esp32#button-actions).
- **The rest**: the buzzer and the green status LED (GPIO 16), the TF card for assets, and the
  battery on GPIO 1, switched by GPIO 40, with deep sleep on battery.

## Setup

1. In the FrameOS backend or [FrameOS Cloud](/guide/cloud), create a frame with **Flash embedded
   device**.
2. Choose **ESP32-S3**, select the **Seeed reTerminal E1003** preset, and enter your WiFi details.
3. Flash it over USB from the browser. Like the rest of the E-series, its USB-C port goes to a
   CH340 UART bridge: on macOS install WCH's
   [CH34x driver](/devices/seeed-reterminal-e1004#macos-install-the-wch-serial-driver-first) first.
4. Install scenes as usual - they sync over WiFi, and the device updates itself over the air.

## Status

🟡 **Should work, not yet confirmed on real hardware.** The pins come from Seeed's examples and
board files; nobody has run FrameOS on an E1003 yet. If you have one, tell us on
[GitHub](https://github.com/FrameOS/frameos/issues/new) or
[Discord](https://discord.gg/9dT9y7EzUw) how it goes.
