The **Seeed reTerminal E1003** is the 10.3" model in the reTerminal E-series: a 1404×1872
**16-level grayscale** panel with touch, an ESP32-S3 with 8&nbsp;MB of PSRAM and 32&nbsp;MB of
flash, a microSD slot, a 3000&nbsp;mAh battery, the E-series keys (a green refresh button and page
up/down), a buzzer, a temperature and humidity sensor and a microphone. Sixteen grays at that
density make it the reading and dashboard frame of the family.

## Status

⚪ **Not supported yet.** The rest of the E-series - the [E1001](/devices/seeed-reterminal-e1001),
[E1002](/devices/seeed-reterminal-e1002) and [E1004](/devices/seeed-reterminal-e1004) - drive SPI
panels that the FrameOS firmware already has drivers for. The E1003's fast-refresh 10.3" panel
needs a different kind of display driver, which the ESP32 firmware doesn't have yet, and touch
input on it isn't wired up either.

Want it? Say so on [GitHub](https://github.com/FrameOS/frameos/issues) or
[Discord](https://discord.gg/9dT9y7EzUw) - it helps us decide what to port next.
