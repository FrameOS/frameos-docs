The **Elecrow CrowPanel 4.2"** e-paper HMI is a small 400×300 black-and-white e-ink board with an
ESP32-S3 (8&nbsp;MB flash, 8&nbsp;MB PSRAM), five keys and a TF slot - the little sibling of the
[CrowPanel 5.79"](/devices/elecrow-crowpanel-5in79), wired the same way.

FrameOS runs on it as a full [ESP32-S3 frame](/guide/esp32) - scenes render on the device. Pick
the **Elecrow CrowPanel 4.2"** hardware preset (`elecrow_crowpanel_epd_4in2`) and the panel, GPIO
map and keys are configured for you.

## What it does

- **Black and white with partial refresh.** FrameOS drives the glass on the waveforms Elecrow's
  own firmware loads: a full refresh when the whole picture changes, and a crisp partial update in
  about 0.6&nbsp;s when part of it does.
- **Five keys**, the same as the 5.79"'s ([button standard](/guide/buttons)): HOME,
  the top key, opens the [scene switcher](/guide/scenes#input), and held, the scene's settings.
  EXIT, the bottom key, is Back, and held it opens the scene switcher too. The rocker walks what's
  on the screen, pressed it's the Action key, and tilted and held it switches to the next or
  previous scene.
- **The power LED** is lit, and the TF slot is set up in the preset.

Elecrow has shipped this board with two different glasses. The preset drives the one on the board
FrameOS was tested on; on a board where it draws nothing, `set panel EPD_4in2_V2` on the
[USB console](/guide/esp32#provisioning-by-hand) picks the driver for the other one (untested).

## Setup

1. In the FrameOS backend or [FrameOS Cloud](/guide/cloud), create a frame with **Flash embedded
   device** - or flash it standalone from [flash.frameos.net](https://flash.frameos.net/).
2. Choose **ESP32-S3**, select the **Elecrow CrowPanel 4.2"** preset, and enter your WiFi details.
3. Flash it over USB from the browser.
4. Install scenes as usual - they sync over WiFi, and the device updates itself over the air.

## Status

🟢 **Confirmed working** - tested on real hardware: the picture, partial updates and the keys. The
TF slot hasn't been tried with a card yet.
