The **XIAO ePaper Display Board EE03** pairs a XIAO ESP32-S3 Plus (8&nbsp;MB of PSRAM, 16&nbsp;MB of
flash) with Seeed's **10.3" 1404×1872 grayscale** panel (40-pin connector), with an on-board IT8951
controller for fast refreshes, an SHT40 temperature sensor for waveform compensation, three user
keys and a battery connector with a charger. It's the DIY counterpart of the
[reTerminal E1003](/devices/seeed-reterminal-e1003), and FrameOS drives its glass the same way,
rendering on the device.

## Setup

1. In the FrameOS backend or [FrameOS Cloud](/guide/cloud), create a frame with **Flash embedded
   device** and choose **ESP32-S3**.
2. Select the **Seeed XIAO ePaper EE03** hardware preset (`seeed_xiao_ee03`). It selects the
   `waveshare.EPD_10in3` driver with the E1003's tuning, the panel power switch, the temperature
   sensor, the three keys and the battery wiring.
3. Flash it over USB from the browser, then install scenes as usual.

## Buttons and battery

The keys are KEY1 (GPIO 2), KEY2 (GPIO 3) and KEY3 (GPIO 5), and they arrive in scenes as
[button events](/guide/scenes#input). Nothing is bound to them out of the box: give them a job under
[button actions](/guide/esp32#button-actions). The battery is read through a 2:1 divider on GPIO 1,
switched on by GPIO 6.

The board can't tell the firmware when USB is plugged in, so the preset leaves deep sleep off. Turn
it on under [Power](/guide/esp32#power) if the frame lives on its battery.

| Signal | GPIO |
| ------ | ---- |
| CS     | 44   |
| MISO   | 8    |
| RST    | 38   |
| HRDY (busy) | 4 |
| SCK    | 7    |
| MOSI   | 9    |
| PWR    | 43   |
| SHT40 SDA / SCL | 42 / 41 |
