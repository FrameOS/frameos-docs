The **XIAO ePaper Display Board EE03** pairs a XIAO ESP32-S3 Plus with Seeed's **10.3"
1404×1872 grayscale** panel (40-pin connector), with an on-board timing controller for fast
refreshes, an SHT40 temperature sensor for waveform compensation, three user keys and a battery
connector with a charger. It's the DIY counterpart of the
[reTerminal E1003](/devices/seeed-reterminal-e1003).

## Status

⚪ **Not supported yet.** The [E1003](/devices/seeed-reterminal-e1003) drives its 10.3" glass
through an IT8951 controller (firmware 2026.10.21+). This board needs a preset of its own, and
nobody has checked yet whether its timing controller works with the same driver.

Want it? Say so on [GitHub](https://github.com/FrameOS/frameos/issues) or
[Discord](https://discord.gg/9dT9y7EzUw) - it helps us decide what to port next.
