The **XIAO ePaper Display Board EE05** is the refreshed, slimmer sibling of the
[EE04](/devices/seeed-xiao-epaper-ee04): the same XIAO ESP32-S3 Plus (8&nbsp;MB of PSRAM), a
**24-pin** panel connector, three side-mounted keys, a battery connector with a hardware power
switch, and an extension header for sensors. It's the board in the
[TRMNL 4.26" DIY Kit](/devices/trmnl-4in26-diy-kit), and Seeed also sells it on its own or as the
XIAO ePaper DIY Kit.

FrameOS runs the **full on-device renderer** on it.

## Setup

1. In the FrameOS backend or [FrameOS Cloud](/guide/cloud), create a frame with **Flash embedded
   device** and choose **ESP32-S3**.
2. Select the **Seeed XIAO ePaper EE05** hardware preset (`seeed_xiao_ee05`). It fills in the pins,
   the panel power switch, the three keys and the battery wiring.
3. Pick the panel you plugged in - the preset stays selected. It starts on the 7.5" black/white,
   and FrameOS Cloud asks for the panel when you flash. Seeed's panels usually share their
   controllers with Waveshare's: pick the driver with the same size, resolution and colors in the
   [device database](/devices).
4. Flash it over USB from the browser, then install scenes as usual.

## Buttons and battery

The keys are KEY1 (GPIO 2), KEY2 (GPIO 3) and KEY3 (GPIO 8), which is not where the
[EE04](/devices/seeed-xiao-epaper-ee04) has its third key. They arrive in scenes as
[button events](/guide/scenes#input). The battery is read through a 2:1 divider on GPIO 1, switched
on by GPIO 39.

The board can't tell the firmware when USB is plugged in, so the preset leaves deep sleep off. Turn
it on under [Power](/guide/esp32#power) if the frame lives on its battery.
