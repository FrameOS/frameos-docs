The **XIAO ePaper Display Board EE05** is the refreshed, slimmer sibling of the
[EE04](/devices/seeed-xiao-epaper-ee04): the same XIAO ESP32-S3 Plus (8&nbsp;MB of PSRAM), a
**24-pin** panel connector, three side-mounted keys, a battery connector with a hardware power
switch, and an extension header for sensors. It's the board in the
[TRMNL 4.26" DIY Kit](/devices/trmnl-4in26-diy-kit), and Seeed also sells it on its own or as the
XIAO ePaper DIY Kit.

FrameOS runs the **full on-device renderer** on it.

## Setup

1. In the FrameOS backend or [FrameOS Cloud](/guide/cloud), create a frame with **Flash embedded
   device** and choose **ESP32-S3**.
2. With the 4.26" panel, select the **TRMNL 4.26" DIY Kit** preset and you're done. With another
   panel, select the preset first for the pins, then pick your panel - the form switches to a
   custom board but keeps them. Seeed's panels usually share their controllers with Waveshare's:
   pick the driver with the same size, resolution and colors in the [device database](/devices).
3. Flash it over USB from the browser, then install scenes as usual.

## Buttons and battery

The keys are on GPIO 2, 3 and 5. The 4.26" preset registers GPIO 2 (plus BOOT); add the others under
**GPIO buttons** in the frame's settings. The battery is read through a 2:1 divider on GPIO 1,
switched on by GPIO 6 - the 4.26" preset carries that wiring, and on a custom board you enter the
three values under **Power**.
