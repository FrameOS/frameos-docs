The **XIAO ePaper Display Board EE04** is Seeed's do-it-yourself e-paper board: a XIAO
ESP32-S3 Plus (8&nbsp;MB of PSRAM) on a carrier with a **24-pin** and a **50-pin** panel
connector (a jumper picks which one is live), three user keys, a reset button and a battery
connector with a charger. Bring your own panel - anything from a 1.54" black/white tag to the
7.5" tri-color and, on the 50-pin connector, the 7.3" Spectra 6.

It is the same XIAO ePaper driver board family the [TRMNL 7.5" DIY Kit](/devices/trmnl-og-diy-kit)
and [TRMNL 4.26" DIY Kit](/devices/trmnl-4in26-diy-kit) are built on, so FrameOS already knows its
wiring and runs the **full on-device renderer** on it.

## Setup

1. In the FrameOS backend or [FrameOS Cloud](/guide/cloud), create a frame with **Flash embedded
   device** and choose **ESP32-S3**.
2. Select the **TRMNL 7.5" DIY Kit** hardware preset - it fills in this board's pins
   (SCK 7, MOSI 9, CS 44, DC 10, RST 38, BUSY 4).
3. With the 7.5" black/white panel, that's it. With another panel, pick it now. The form switches to a custom board but keeps the pins.
   Seeed's panels usually share their controllers with Waveshare's: pick the driver with the same
   size, resolution and colors in the [device database](/devices) - for example the
   [7.3" Spectra 6](/devices/waveshare-epd-7in3e) on the 50-pin connector.
4. Flash it over USB from the browser, then install scenes as usual.

## Buttons and battery

The keys are on GPIO 2, 3 and 5. The preset registers GPIO 5 (plus BOOT); add the other two under
**GPIO buttons** in the frame's settings and they arrive in scenes as button events. The battery
is read through a 2:1 divider on GPIO 1, switched on by GPIO 6. The TRMNL presets carry that
wiring; on a custom board enter the three values under **Power** so the frame shows its charge and
the [deep sleep options](/guide/esp32#power) apply.
