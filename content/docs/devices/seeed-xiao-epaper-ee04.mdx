The **XIAO ePaper Display Board EE04** is Seeed's do-it-yourself e-paper board: a XIAO
ESP32-S3 Plus (8&nbsp;MB of PSRAM) on a carrier with a **24-pin** and a **50-pin** panel
connector (a jumper picks which one is live), three user keys, a reset button and a battery
connector with a charger. Bring your own panel - anything from a 1.54" black/white tag to the
7.5" tri-color and, on the 50-pin connector, the 7.3" Spectra 6.

It has a hardware preset of its own, and FrameOS runs the **full on-device renderer** on it.

## Setup

1. In the FrameOS backend or [FrameOS Cloud](/guide/cloud), create a frame with **Flash embedded
   device** and choose **ESP32-S3**.
2. Select the **Seeed XIAO ePaper EE04** hardware preset (`seeed_xiao_ee04`). It fills in the pins,
   the panel power switch, the three keys and the battery wiring.
3. Pick the panel you plugged in - the preset stays selected. It starts on the 7.5" black/white,
   and FrameOS Cloud asks for the panel when you flash. Seeed's panels usually share their
   controllers with Waveshare's: pick the driver with the same size, resolution and colors in the
   [device database](/devices) - for example the
   [7.3" Spectra 6](/devices/waveshare-epd-7in3e) on the 50-pin connector.
4. Flash it over USB from the browser, then install scenes as usual.

## Buttons and battery

The keys are KEY1 (GPIO 2), KEY2 (GPIO 3) and KEY3 (GPIO 5), and they arrive in scenes as
[button events](/guide/scenes#input). The battery is read through a 2:1 divider on GPIO 1, switched
on by GPIO 6.

The board can't tell the firmware when USB is plugged in, so the preset leaves deep sleep off. Turn
it on under [Power](/guide/esp32#power) if the frame lives on its battery.
