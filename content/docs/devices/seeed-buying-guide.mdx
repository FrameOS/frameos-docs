Seeed Studio makes the closest thing to a ready-made FrameOS frame. The **reTerminal E-series**
puts an ESP32-S3 with 8&nbsp;MB of PSRAM, an e-ink panel, buttons, a buzzer and a battery in a
finished case, and FrameOS has a hardware preset for each model: pick it, flash from the browser,
and scenes render on the device itself. For your own builds, Seeed's XIAO ePaper boards carry
the same chip.

This page covers every Seeed ESP32 display we know of, including the ones FrameOS doesn't
support yet.

## Which one should I buy?

- **An art frame for photos and paintings**: the [E1004](/devices/seeed-reterminal-e1004). A
  13.3" six-color panel behind a mat in a real picture frame. It renders 1200×1600 scenes on its
  8&nbsp;MB of PSRAM, and on battery it deep-sleeps between refreshes.
- **Color, but smaller**: the [E1002](/devices/seeed-reterminal-e1002). The 7.3" Spectra 6 glass
  is the same panel as the [Waveshare 7.3" (E)](/devices/waveshare-epd-7in3e) and the
  [PhotoPainter](/devices/waveshare-esp32-s3-photopainter), in a finished case with three buttons.
- **Calendars, agendas, dashboards**: the [E1001](/devices/seeed-reterminal-e1001). Black and
  white is crisp, and it refreshes in seconds where a color panel takes much longer.
- **A desk or fridge note**: the [Sticky](/devices/seeed-reterminal-sticky). 800×480 on a
  3.97" panel makes small text sharp.
- **Your own frame, your own case**: one of the boards below and a panel.

For anything else, the bare [XIAO ESP32-S3](https://www.seeedstudio.com/XIAO-ESP32S3-p-5627.html)
(8&nbsp;MB of PSRAM) is the reference board for a [custom ESP32 build](/guide/esp32#supported-boards):
wire it to any Waveshare-family SPI panel, pick the panel and the pins, and flash. Seeed's
nRF52840 boards (EN04, EN05) are not ESP32s and don't run FrameOS.

## Before you buy

- **The preset does the setup.** Choosing a reTerminal preset fills in the panel, pins, buttons,
  buzzer, battery wiring and flash size - see the [ESP32 guide](/guide/esp32#flash-the-device) for
  the whole flow, from the backend or [FrameOS Cloud](/guide/cloud).
- **macOS needs a driver first.** The reTerminal E-series talks USB through a WCH CH340 chip, which
  macOS doesn't recognise out of the box. Install WCH's driver before flashing - the
  [E1004 page](/devices/seeed-reterminal-e1004#macos-install-the-wch-serial-driver-first) has the
  steps. Windows 11 and Linux work as they are.
- **The buttons and buzzer work in scenes.** The three E-series keys arrive as button events a
  scene can react to, and the buzzer clicks on each press.
- **Battery frames sleep.** The E1004 preset deep-sleeps on battery and wakes every 15 minutes or
  on a button, and the backend or cloud shows when it's due back - see
  [battery and deep sleep](/guide/esp32#power). The battery reading is verified on the E1004 and
  wired the same way on the E1001 and E1002 according to Seeed's schematics.
- **Color takes time.** A Spectra 6 refresh is about half a minute on the 13.3" panel. Perfect for
  a frame that changes a few times an hour, not for a clock with a second hand.
- **FrameOS replaces the factory firmware.** Flashing it overwrites the firmware the device ships
  with, and the frame then talks to your own backend or FrameOS Cloud - no other account needed.
