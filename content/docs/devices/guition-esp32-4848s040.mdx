The **Guition ESP32-4848S040** is a 4" square 480×480 IPS **touch LCD** made to fit a European
wall box (the "86 box" smart-home panel), with an ESP32-S3: 16&nbsp;MB of flash, 8&nbsp;MB of
PSRAM and GT911 touch. Its glass is the same as the
[SenseCAP Indicator](/devices/seeed-sensecap-indicator)'s, wired differently, so it needs its own
preset rather than the bare panel.

## What it does

Pick the **Guition ESP32-4848S040** hardware preset (`guition_esp32_4848s040`) and it behaves like
the other [LCD boards](/guide/esp32#lcd):

- **Scenes animate**, drawn straight into the LCD's frame buffer with only the changed part
  redrawn.
- **Touch** reaches scenes as taps, drags, swipes and pointer events
  ([input events](/guide/scenes#input)).
- **The edges are its keys.** The board has no button, so the frame is driven by edge swipes: up
  from the bottom for the scene switcher, down from the top for the scene's settings, and in from
  the left or right for back and forward.
- **It boots straight into its scenes** and joins WiFi in the background; set the network on the
  screen (swipe down, tap the gear). Flashed with nothing picked, it opens on the scene switcher.

## What it doesn't

- **The microSD slot isn't supported yet.** Assets live in the board's internal storage.

## Setup

1. In the FrameOS backend or [FrameOS Cloud](/guide/cloud), create a frame with **Flash embedded
   device** - or flash it standalone from [flash.frameos.net](https://flash.frameos.net/).
2. Choose **ESP32-S3**, select the **Guition ESP32-4848S040** preset, and enter your WiFi details.
3. Flash it over USB from the browser (its USB port is a CH340 serial bridge).
4. Install scenes as usual - they sync over WiFi, and the device updates itself over the air.
