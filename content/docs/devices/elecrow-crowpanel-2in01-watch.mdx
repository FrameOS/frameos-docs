The **Elecrow CrowPanel 2.01" watch** is an ESP32-S3 smartwatch board (16&nbsp;MB flash,
8&nbsp;MB PSRAM) with a 240×296 IPS **touch LCD**, a crown, a PDM microphone, a vibration motor, a
clock chip and a battery. It's the smallest screen FrameOS draws on, and the frame's own screens -
the scene list, the scene settings and the frame page - fit it with every word on the glass.

## What it does

Pick the **Elecrow CrowPanel 2.01" watch** hardware preset (`elecrow_crowpanel_watch_2in01`):

- **Scenes animate and answer a touch.** It's an [LCD-class screen](/guide/esp32#lcd): the panel
  keeps its own picture and is sent only the part that changed.
- **Touch** reaches scenes as taps, drags, swipes and pointer events
  ([input events](/guide/scenes#input)), and a swipe in from an edge is the frame's: up for the
  scene switcher, down for the scene's settings, left and right for back and forward.
- **The crown** ([button standard](/guide/buttons)): each click of a turn is Next or
  Prev, walking what's on the screen - or, on a scene with nothing to press, its own forward and
  back. Pushed, it's the Action key, and held it's the [voice chat](/guide/esp32#voice-chat) key,
  through the watch's microphone.
- **The keys**: the power key opens the scene switcher, and BOOT turns the screen off or on - held,
  it opens the scene switcher too.
- **Brightness and dimming.** The backlight dims: set **Brightness**, **Dim when idle** and
  **Dimmed brightness** under [Power](/guide/settings#power-and-battery), or drag the Brightness
  bar on the frame's own page.
- **The vibration motor** plays the notes a buzzer would, so a press is felt on the wrist.
- **The battery's charge** shows in the backend or cloud, and the clock chip sets the time at boot,
  before WiFi is up.

The speaker and the accelerometer aren't used yet.

## Setup

1. In the FrameOS backend or [FrameOS Cloud](/guide/cloud), create a frame with **Flash embedded
   device** - or flash it standalone from [flash.frameos.net](https://flash.frameos.net/).
2. Choose **ESP32-S3**, select the **Elecrow CrowPanel 2.01" watch** preset, and enter your WiFi
   details.
3. Flash it over USB from the browser. A touch board sets up on its own screen: swipe down from
   the top, tap the gear, and join a WiFi network there.
4. Install scenes as usual - they sync over WiFi, and the device updates itself over the air.
