The **Elecrow CrowPanel 5.79"** is a wide 792×272 black-and-white e-ink board with an ESP32-S3
(8&nbsp;MB flash, 8&nbsp;MB PSRAM). The glass is driven by two controllers side by side; FrameOS
handles that for you. Its shape suits a status strip - departures, a now-playing bar, a single-line
dashboard.

FrameOS runs on it as a full [ESP32-S3 frame](/guide/esp32) - scenes render on the device. Pick
the **Elecrow CrowPanel 5.79"** hardware preset (`elecrow_crowpanel_5in79`) and the panel, GPIO map
and all five keys are configured for you.

## What it does

- **Black and white with partial refresh.** FrameOS drives the glass on its own waveforms, as
  Elecrow does, so white is white, and a change that touches part of the picture updates only that
  part, without the full-screen flash.
- **Five keys** ([button standard](/guide/buttons)): HOME, the top key, opens the
  [scene switcher](/guide/scenes#input), and held, the scene's settings. EXIT, the bottom key, is
  Back - the scene's page or photo before, or closing the settings - and held it opens the scene
  switcher too. The rocker walks what's on the screen, pressed it's the Action key, and tilted and
  held it switches to the next or previous scene.
- **The TF slot** holds the frame's [assets](/guide/esp32#assets-and-the-sd-card) and fonts, and
  the power LED is lit.

## Setup

1. In the FrameOS backend or [FrameOS Cloud](/guide/cloud), create a frame with **Flash embedded
   device** - or flash it standalone from [flash.frameos.net](https://flash.frameos.net/).
2. Choose **ESP32-S3**, select the **Elecrow CrowPanel 5.79"** preset, and enter your WiFi details.
3. Flash it over USB from the browser - the release image for the board is written and the board
   set up as this frame.
4. Install scenes as usual - they sync over WiFi, and the device updates itself over the air.
