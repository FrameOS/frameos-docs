The **XIAO ePaper Display Board EE02** is Seeed's DIY route to a big color frame: a XIAO
ESP32-S3 Plus on a carrier made for the **13.3" Spectra 6** 1200×1600 panel (60-pin connector),
with refresh, previous and next keys, a reset and a boot button, and a battery connector with a
charger and power switch. Buy it with the panel and you have the parts of a
[reTerminal E1004](/devices/seeed-reterminal-e1004) to put in a frame of your own.

## Status

⚪ **Not supported yet - but close.** The firmware already drives 13.3" Spectra 6 panels on an
8&nbsp;MB ESP32-S3, rendering on-device on the 16-bit canvas: that's what the E1004 and the
[Waveshare 13.3E6](/devices/waveshare-esp32-s3-epaper-13in3e6) do. What's missing is a hardware
preset with this board's pin map, buttons and battery wiring, checked on a real board.

Want it? Say so on [GitHub](https://github.com/FrameOS/frameos/issues) or
[Discord](https://discord.gg/9dT9y7EzUw) - it helps us decide what to port next.
