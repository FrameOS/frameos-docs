The **XIAO ePaper Display Board EE02** is Seeed's DIY route to a big color frame: a XIAO
ESP32-S3 Plus (8&nbsp;MB of PSRAM, 16&nbsp;MB of flash) on a carrier made for the **13.3" Spectra 6**
1200×1600 panel (60-pin connector), with three keys, a reset and a boot button, and a battery
connector with a charger and power switch. Buy it with the panel and you have the parts of a
[reTerminal E1004](/devices/seeed-reterminal-e1004) to put in a frame of your own.

FrameOS runs the **full on-device renderer** on it, on the same 16-bit canvas as the E1004.

## Setup

1. In the FrameOS backend or [FrameOS Cloud](/guide/cloud), create a frame with **Flash embedded
   device** and choose **ESP32-S3**.
2. Select the **Seeed XIAO ePaper EE02** hardware preset (`seeed_xiao_ee02`). It selects the
   `waveshare.EPD_13in3e` driver with the T133A01 refresh sequence this glass wants, the panel's
   second chip select, the panel power switch, the three keys and the battery wiring.
3. Flash it over USB from the browser, then install scenes as usual.

A full refresh on a panel this size takes around half a minute.

## Buttons and battery

The keys are KEY1 (GPIO 2), KEY2 (GPIO 3) and KEY3 (GPIO 5), and they arrive in scenes as
[button events](/guide/scenes#input). Nothing is bound to them out of the box: give them a job under
[button actions](/guide/esp32#button-actions). The battery is read through a 2:1 divider on GPIO 1,
switched on by GPIO 6.

The board can't tell the firmware when USB is plugged in, so the preset leaves deep sleep off. Turn
it on under [Power](/guide/esp32#power) if the frame lives on its battery.
