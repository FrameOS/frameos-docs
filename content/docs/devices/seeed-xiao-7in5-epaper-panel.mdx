The **XIAO 7.5" ePaper Panel** is a finished desk display: a 7.5" 800×480 black/white panel in a
white case with a stand, driven by a XIAO ESP32-C3, with a 2000&nbsp;mAh battery that Seeed rates at
about three months in deep sleep.

## Status

⚪ **Not supported yet.** The pieces exist - FrameOS drives 7.5" 800×480 panels, and PSRAM-less
ESP32-C3 boards like the [TRMNL OG](/devices/trmnl-og) run as thin clients that the
[backend](/guide/backend) renders for - but there's no hardware preset with this board's pin map
and battery wiring yet. If you need 7.5" black/white today, the
[reTerminal E1001](/devices/seeed-reterminal-e1001) and the
[TRMNL 7.5" DIY Kit](/devices/trmnl-og-diy-kit) render on the device itself.

Want it? Say so on [GitHub](https://github.com/FrameOS/frameos/issues) or
[Discord](https://discord.gg/9dT9y7EzUw) - it helps us decide what to port next.
