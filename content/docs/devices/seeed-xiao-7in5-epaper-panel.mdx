The **XIAO 7.5" ePaper Panel** is a finished desk display: a 7.5" 800×480 black/white panel in a
white case with a stand, driven by a XIAO ESP32-C3, with a 2000&nbsp;mAh battery that Seeed rates at
about three months in deep sleep.

The C3 has no PSRAM, so FrameOS runs it as a **thin client**: your [backend](/guide/backend) renders
the scene and the board draws it, the same way as the [TRMNL OG](/devices/trmnl-og). FrameOS Cloud
can't render for thin clients yet, so this one needs a self-hosted backend. If you want a 7.5"
black/white frame that renders by itself, the [reTerminal E1001](/devices/seeed-reterminal-e1001) and
the [TRMNL 7.5" DIY Kit](/devices/trmnl-og-diy-kit) do.

## Setup

1. In the FrameOS backend, create a frame with **Flash embedded device**.
2. Choose **ESP32-C3**, select the **Seeed XIAO 7.5" ePaper Panel** preset (`seeed_xiao_epaper_7in5`)
   and enter your WiFi details.
3. Flash it over USB from the browser, then install scenes as usual.

The BOOT key (GPIO 9) arrives in scenes as a button event. FrameOS doesn't read this board's battery.
