The **Elecrow CrowPanel Advance 7.0"** is a 7" 800×480 IPS **touch LCD** with an
ESP32-S3-WROOM-1 on the back: 16&nbsp;MB of flash, 8&nbsp;MB of PSRAM, a GT911 touch panel and a
clock chip. FrameOS supports hardware version 1.2.

## What it does

Pick the **Elecrow CrowPanel Advance 7.0"** hardware preset (`elecrow_crowpanel_advance_7`) and it
behaves like the other [LCD boards](/guide/esp32#lcd):

- **Scenes animate**: a scene whose render interval is under a second renders back to back, drawn
  straight into the LCD's frame buffer with only the changed part redrawn, and the glass doesn't
  flicker.
- **Touch** reaches scenes as taps, drags, swipes and pointer events
  ([input events](/guide/scenes#input)), so a [JSX](/guide/apps#jsx-uis) dashboard or remote
  answers a finger.
- **The BOOT key and the edges** come bound to the frame
  ([button standard](/guide/buttons)): BOOT pressed turns the screen off or on, held
  it opens the scene switcher; swipe up from the bottom for the switcher, down from the top for the
  scene's settings, and in from the left or right for back and forward.
- **The clock chip** sets the time at boot, before WiFi is up.
- **It boots straight into its scenes** and joins WiFi in the background; set the network on the
  screen (swipe down, tap the gear).

## Not driven yet

The microphone, the speaker and the TF slot share pins behind the board's DIP switches and aren't
used yet. Hardware version 1.3 and later talk to their backlight differently and haven't been
tried.

## Setup

1. In the FrameOS backend or [FrameOS Cloud](/guide/cloud), create a frame with **Flash embedded
   device** - or flash it standalone from [flash.frameos.net](https://flash.frameos.net/).
2. Choose **ESP32-S3**, select the **Elecrow CrowPanel Advance 7.0"** preset, and enter your WiFi
   details.
3. Flash it over USB from the browser.
4. Install scenes as usual - they sync over WiFi, and the device updates itself over the air.
